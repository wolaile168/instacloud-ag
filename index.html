Take Action: The 3 Rs

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle — three simple words that can change the world.

🛍️

Reduce

Use less from the start. Carry a reusable bag and bottle, buy only what you need, and say no to single-use plastics.

♻️

Reuse

Give things a second life. Repair instead of replacing, donate what you no longer need, and choose reusable products.

🗑️

Recycle

Sort your waste correctly. Paper, glass, metal, and plastic can all be turned into new products when recycled properly.

🌱

Plant & Protect

Plant trees, protect forests, and support green spaces in your community. One tree can absorb up to 22 kg of CO₂ per year.

💡

Save Energy

Turn off lights when you leave a room, use energy-efficient appliances, and walk, cycle, or take public transport.

💧

Save Water

Fix leaking taps, take shorter showers, and never waste clean water. Every drop counts.

10 Easy Things You Can Do Today

  • Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth.
  • Bring your own shopping bag and water bottle.
  • Switch to LED light bulbs at home.
  • Walk or ride a bike for short trips.
  • Sort your rubbish and recycle properly.
  • Eat less meat and waste less food.
  • Unplug chargers when they are not in use.
  • Plant a tree or grow a small plant.
  • Pick up litter when you see it in nature.
  • Share what you learn — inspire others to act.
